Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Safe Bloom's algorithms, local encryption, pregnancy companion mode, and backup systems.

Your data is stored exclusively on your physical phone inside an encrypted SQLCipher database with 256-bit AES encryption. Safe Bloom has no cloud servers, no central databases, and no remote user accounts. Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export an encrypted backup file.

No. Safe Bloom contains zero third-party analytics trackers, zero advertising SDKs, and zero telemetry. The encryption keys are generated inside your device's hardware Secure Element (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain) and are completely inaccessible to developers, servers, or advertising networks.

The Emergency Nuclear Purge is a safety feature accessible inside Settings. In high-risk situations where phone access might be compelled, tapping Nuclear Purge immediately destroys the hardware encryption keys, cryptographically deletes the SQLite database, wipes local caches, and cancels all scheduled notification alarms in less than a second.

Safe Bloom utilizes a dynamic moving-average algorithm that continuously learns from your confirmed historical cycles. It maps your reproductive month into a 4-phase hormonal timeline: Menstrual, Follicular, Ovulatory (peak 6-day fertile window), and Luteal phases.

Safe Bloom provides a dedicated Spotting logging classification. Unlike other apps that mistakenly treat mid-cycle spotting as the start of a whole new period and distort your cycle averages, Safe Bloom isolates spotting as an informational symptom without altering your predictive timeline.

Activating Pregnancy Mode transforms the app into a prenatal tracking companion. It calculates gestational age (weeks and days) from your Last Menstrual Period (LMP) or conception date, projects Estimated Due Dates (EDD) using Naegele's rule, provides weekly fruit/veggie size milestones, and automatically suppresses menstrual alarms for the duration of the pregnancy.

Navigate to Settings ➔ Export Encrypted Backup. Enter a secure passphrase. Safe Bloom generates a zero-knowledge encrypted JSON vault (using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 and AES-GCM). Move this file to your new device via USB, local sharing, or SD card, then tap Restore from Backup on the Welcome Screen.

No. All reminders (period predictions, ovulation alerts, hydration prompts, daily symptom logging) are scheduled directly with your device's native operating system alarm clock manager. Safe Bloom does not use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM), Apple APNs, or any external push servers.

Discreet Mode allows you to mask sensitive reproductive wording on your phone's lock screen. Instead of notifications displaying terms like "Period Predicted Tomorrow" or "Peak Ovulation Window", Discreet Mode displays neutral, gentle self-care prompts (e.g. "Safe Bloom: Daily health check-in time").
