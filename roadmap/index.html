🚀 Product Roadmap & Vision

Building the Future of Private Health

Safe Bloom is built in public with the community. Discover what we've shipped, what is currently in active development, and submit your own ideas to our wishlist.

🌸 Version 1.0.0 — Production Release

Shipped & Stable

The foundational release establishing our Zero-Knowledge offline architecture and core tracking suite.

  • Hardware-backed SQLCipher AES-256 encrypted SQLite database
  • Dynamic moving-average cycle and 4-phase hormonal prediction engine
  • Try-to-Conceive (TTC) & Full Pregnancy Companion mode with Naegele's rule EDD
  • Daily wellness, symptom, mood, and water intake tracker
  • 100% on-device OS local notifications with discreet lock screen masking
  • Medical-grade offline Ob/Gyn doctor consultation PDF report generator
  • Biometric authentication (Fingerprint / Face ID) and `FLAG_SECURE` screen privacy
  • Encrypted PBKDF2/AES-GCM JSON vault backup and restore
  • Emergency one-tap cryptographic Nuclear Purge
●

⚡ Version 1.1.0 — Ecosystem & Basal Body Temp

In Active Development

Expanding clinical fertility tracking and open distribution channels.

  • F-Droid Inclusion: Packaging and reproducible build metadata for the official F-Droid repository
  • Basal Body Temperature (BBT): Daily thermal logging with biphasic temperature shift visualization
  • Custom Symptoms: Ability to define custom tags, remedies, and medication trackers
  • Multi-Language Support: Localizations for Spanish, French, German, and Portuguese
○

🍎 Version 1.2.0 — iOS & Wearables

Planned

Bringing zero-knowledge privacy to iOS devices and quick smartwatch logging.

  • iOS App Store Release: Full parity release for iPhone utilizing iOS Keychain hardware encryption
  • Wear OS Companion: Minimal offline watch tile for 1-tap hydration and symptom logging
  • Data Portability: Standardized offline CSV export for independent medical spreadsheet analysis
