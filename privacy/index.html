🔒 Zero-Knowledge Privacy Architecture

Privacy Policy & Security Guarantee

Safe Bloom does not have a traditional privacy policy because we do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any of your data.

🌸 The 3-Sentence Summary

  1. Safe Bloom operates 100% offline on your physical device with zero backend servers or user accounts.
  2. All your reproductive data is encrypted at rest using SQLCipher AES-256 with encryption keys anchored in your device's hardware Keystore.
  3. We include zero analytics SDKs, zero advertising trackers, and zero telemetry.

1. Zero Cloud Servers & No Remote Accounts

When you open Safe Bloom, you will never be asked for an email address, phone number, Google account, or password. There is no remote server database where your cycles, sexual encounters, symptoms, or pregnancy milestones could ever be stored or leaked.

Even if a government agency, advertiser, or malicious actor served a subpoena or warrant to the Safe Bloom project developers, we have nothing to provide because we hold zero user data.

2. Hardware-Backed SQLCipher AES-256 Encryption

Unlike standard applications that store records in plain SQLite files, Safe Bloom encrypts the entire SQLite database file page-by-page using SQLCipher with 256-bit Advanced Encryption Standard (AES-256).

🔐 Key Management Architecture

  • Hardware Root of Trust: Cryptographic master keys are generated inside the Android Keystore / iOS Keychain hardware security module (TEE/SE).
  • Zero Persistent Plaintext: Keys reside solely in encrypted transient memory while the app is actively in use and unlocked.
  • Biometric Protection: Optional biometric authentication (Fingerprint / Face ID) prevents unauthorized physical access.

3. The Emergency Nuclear Purge Protocol

In high-risk or emergency situations where physical access to your phone may be compelled or seized, Safe Bloom provides a single-tap Emergency Nuclear Purge inside Settings.

Executing a Nuclear Purge performs:

  • Cryptographic zeroization and immediate destruction of hardware Keystore encryption keys.
  • Permanent file deletion of the local SQLCipher database, log caches, and temporary files.
  • Instant cancellation of all scheduled local notification alarms.
  • Immediate app state reset to uninitialized state.

5. Device Permissions Breakdown

Safe Bloom requests only the bare minimum native OS permissions required to function:

🔔 Notifications & Alarms

POST_NOTIFICATIONS & SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Used strictly to trigger on-device reminders for periods, fertility, and hydration. Reminders run locally on the phone's clock without remote servers.

🛡️ Biometrics

USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT: Used to verify identity on app launch or when switching back from the background. Biometric templates never leave the OS secure enclave.

6. Open Source Verifiability

You do not have to take our word for it. The complete codebase for Safe Bloom is open source under the MIT License. Anyone can audit our code, verify that no network requests are made, or build the application from source.
