Self-Help Guides

Common Solutions & How-Tos

💾 How to Backup & Restore Your Data

Because Safe Bloom has zero cloud servers, moving to a new phone requires exporting an encrypted vault file:

  1. Open Safe Bloom ➔ Settings ➔ Export Encrypted Backup.
  2. Choose a strong, memorable passphrase (used for PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 derivation).
  3. Save the resulting safebloom_backup_*.json file to an SD card, USB drive, or transfer via local Bluetooth.
  4. On your new phone, install Safe Bloom and tap Restore from Backup on the Welcome Screen.
⚠️ Important: We do not have your passphrase. If you forget it, the backup cannot be decrypted.

🔔 Ensuring Notification Reliability

Since Safe Bloom triggers alarms strictly on-device without cloud push servers, aggressive Android battery managers can sometimes delay reminders:

  1. Go to Android Settings ➔ Apps ➔ Safe Bloom ➔ Battery.
  2. Select Unrestricted (or disable "Battery Optimization" / "Put app to sleep").
  3. Ensure Allow Exact Alarms permission is granted under App Permissions.
  4. Open Safe Bloom ➔ Settings ➔ Notification Diagnostics to verify alarm scheduling.
💡 Pro-Tip: On Samsung (OneUI) or Xiaomi (MIUI), make sure Safe Bloom is excluded from "Auto-Sleep Apps".

📊 Generating Doctor Consultation Reports

Need to share cycle regularity or symptom logs with your gynecologist or healthcare provider?

  1. Open Safe Bloom ➔ tap Reports & Analytics.
  2. Select the date range (e.g. Last 6 Months, Past Year, or Full History).
  3. Tap Generate Ob/Gyn Summary (PDF).
  4. The PDF is rendered 100% offline on your device and can be printed or exported to your preferred document viewer.

🛡️ Biometric Lock & Screenshot Privacy

Safe Bloom includes system-level controls to prevent unauthorized observation:

  1. Biometric Lock: Enable in Settings ➔ Security to require Fingerprint or Face ID whenever the app opens.
  2. Screen Security (`FLAG_SECURE`): Blocks screenshots, screen recorders, and hides the app preview in the Android App Switcher.
  3. Discreet Lock Screen Mode: Replaces cycle terms with neutral health check-in wording on lock screen notifications.

🔒 Responsible Security Disclosure

If you discover a cryptographic or security vulnerability in Safe Bloom, please report it responsibly via GitHub Security Advisories or by emailing our core maintainers.
