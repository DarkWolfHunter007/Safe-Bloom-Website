Zero-Knowledge Guarantee

Hardware-Backed Cryptography In Your Pocket

Safe Bloom never sends unencrypted bytes anywhere. Database keys are generated inside your phone's hardware Secure Element (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain) and never leave memory.

  • SQLCipher AES-256: The entire database file on disk is encrypted at rest.
  • Air-Gapped Portability: Backups are encrypted with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 and AES-GCM before you save or move them.
  • Emergency Nuclear Purge: Deletes keys, logs, and database files instantly with cryptographic wiping.
